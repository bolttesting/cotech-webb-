How to use them on the site

  1. Quickest option (stills): copy the .jpg files into /images, overwriting the placeholders. No code changes are needed.
  2. Animated with no layout change: change .jpg to .gif in the <img src>. GIFs are 0.7× resolution (648 px wide) to keep them light.
  3. Best quality and speed (recommended): replace the <img> with a looping video. MP4/WebM files are about 0.4–1.2 MB, full resolution, and much smaller than the GIFs.
